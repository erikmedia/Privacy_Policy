Drivana Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026 · Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Drivana mobile application (“Drivana”, “the App”) handles information, and it applies to everyone who uses the App. Drivana is an offline-first, multi-stop route planning, optimization, and driver workflow app that helps you plan routes, navigate, and manage driving-related tasks.

By “we”, “us”, and “our” we mean the developer and operator of the Drivana App. We designed Drivana so that your personal data — above all your location and your destinations — stays on your own device. This policy describes in full what little data the App touches, what it is used for, and who (if anyone) it is shared with.

This policy is provided in English and applies worldwide, without geographic restriction and without any login or registration requirement.

2. Summary of key points

  • Location stays on your device. Location is used only while the App is open and in use, only for features you start (navigation, route tracking, ETAs), and is processed on your device. We do not transmit your location to our servers.
  • No account, no tracking, no ads. Drivana has no user accounts or login. It contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs from third parties, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • Your data is stored locally. Routes, saved places, stops, settings, and technical logs are stored only in the App’s local database on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes all of them.
  • The only network traffic is map content. When you download offline map packages, the App contacts our download servers. Those downloads contain map and road data only — not your identity, not your location, not your routes.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data. There are no third parties with whom your personal data is shared for commercial purposes.

3. Information the App collects and processes

3.1 Precise and approximate location

With your explicit permission (Android location permission), Drivana accesses your device’s precise (GPS) and approximate (network-based) location. This happens only while the App is in the foreground and in use; the App does not request or use background location.

Purpose. Location is used solely to operate the features you invoke: showing your position on the map, turn-by-turn guidance, multi-stop route optimization, distance and arrival-time (ETA) calculations, and diagnostics screens that report GPS status.

Processing on-device. Route calculation and position handling are performed by a routing engine that runs entirely on your device. Your location is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Local logging. Some GPS-related technical events (for example, GPS signal loss or fix quality shown on the diagnostics screen) may be recorded in the App’s local database so the App and its diagnostics tools can function. These records remain on your device and are never transmitted.

3.2 Information you enter

The App stores information you type in or choose, such as saved places (for example a home address), stops and destinations, route notes, trip settings, and preferences. This information exists only in the App’s local database on your device and is used only to provide the App’s features to you.

3.3 Offline map packages

Drivana can download offline map packages (map tiles and road-network routing data) to your device. Downloading happens over an encrypted HTTPS connection from servers operated by or for us. The request itself contains no personal data about you — no identity, no account (there are none), no precise location, and no history of the places you visit. If you never download a map package, the App makes no internet connection at all.

3.4 Technical and diagnostic information (on-device only)

The App keeps local technical logs — for example routing-engine events, map-package integrity checks, and GPS state — to power its built-in diagnostics screens and to recover from errors. These logs stay on your device. We do not receive copies. Crash or stability reports may be collected by the operating system or the app store platform (for example Google Play) under that platform’s own privacy policy; we do not add any third-party crash-reporting service to the App.

3.5 What we do NOT collect

  • No name, e-mail address, phone number, or any account identifier (the App has no registration).
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • No advertising identifiers, no advertising, and no marketing profiles.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics sent to us or to third parties.
  • No background location tracking, and no location collection when the permission is denied or revoked.

4. Permissions the App uses

Permission When Why
Precise location (fine) Only while the App is open and in use Map display of your position, turn-by-turn navigation, route optimization, ETAs, GPS diagnostics
Approximate location (coarse) Only while the App is open and in use Fallback positioning when precise GPS is unavailable

No other sensitive permissions are used. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device’s system settings; the App will then continue to work in planning mode without live positioning.

5. How we use information

All processing described in this policy has one purpose: to make the App work for you. Specifically:

  • To display your position and render the map on your screen.
  • To calculate, optimize, and navigate multi-stop routes and to estimate arrival times.
  • To store the places, stops, and settings you enter so the App remembers them between uses.
  • To download and verify offline map packages that you request.
  • To diagnose technical problems locally through the App’s diagnostics screens.

We do not use your information for profiling, behavioral advertising, automated decisions about you, or any purpose unrelated to providing the App’s functionality.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties. Because Drivana has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, there is no data pipeline from the App to us containing your personal data. The only parties that ever handle information relating to your use of the App are:

  • You and your device. All personal data (location, places, routes, logs) is processed and stored on your device.
  • The operating system and app store platform (for example Google LLC for Android devices and Google Play). The platform provides the App, its updates, and standard operating-system services such as GPS, and may process standard device, crash, and performance data under its own privacy policy.
  • Download-service providers. Off-the-shelf hosting/CDN infrastructure may be used to serve the map-package files described in section 3.3. Those services deliver public map data only; they receive no personal data about you from the App.
  • Legal authorities. We may disclose information only if compelled by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process. Because personal data stays on your device, there is in practice no server-side store of your data for us to disclose.

We will never transfer your personal data in connection with a sale, merger, or acquisition without notice to you and without ensuring this policy continues to protect it (or notifying you before it applies under a different policy).

7. Data storage, security, retention, and deletion

Storage location

Personal data is stored exclusively in the App’s private, sandboxed local database on your device. It is not stored on our servers, because we operate no servers that receive it.

Security

The App relies on the operating system’s app sandboxing to protect local data, and uses encrypted HTTPS connections for any downloads it makes. Location access requires an explicit runtime permission that you can revoke at any time.

Retention and deletion

Your data is retained only for as long as the App is installed on your device. You can delete it at any time:

  • Delete individual items (saved places, stops, routes) inside the App.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the App, which permanently removes the local database, all logs, and downloaded map packages from your device.
  • Stop location processing by revoking the location permission, or by using the App’s planning features without starting navigation.

8. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Drivana displays no advertising, includes no analytics or tracking libraries, and does not collect or use data for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal data, and we have never sold personal data.

9. Children’s privacy

Drivana is a general-audience route planning and navigation tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the App has no accounts or registration, we have no way to identify users, and no personal data leaves the device in any case. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will address it.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or other applicable laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because Drivana’s design keeps all personal data on your own device, you can exercise nearly all of these rights directly and immediately: the data is visible in the App, deletable in the App, and fully removable by uninstalling. If you need anything further, write to us at the address below and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. There is no account to delete because there are no accounts.

You may also control the App’s access to location at any time through your device’s system settings.

11. International data handling

Drivana can be used anywhere in the world, and its privacy design is location-independent: personal data never leaves your device, so no international transfers of your personal data by us take place. The only cross-border data movement associated with the App is the ordinary delivery of downloadable map data and app updates through global hosting and app-store infrastructure, none of which involves your personal data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when App functionality changes. The effective date at the top of this page always shows the current version. If we make a material change, we will announce it in a prominent way — such as an in-app notice or an update to the App’s store listing — before it takes effect. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.

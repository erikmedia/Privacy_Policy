QuoteCentral Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Applies to the QuoteCentral app on Google Play.

The short version

  • QuoteCentral has no servers and no accounts. Your clients, quotes, invoices, payments, and business profile are stored only on your device, in the app's private database.
  • The app makes no internet connections of its own. Nothing about you, your clients, or your money is transmitted to us, because there is no us to transmit it to — the developer never receives a copy of anything you record.
  • Your documents are printed on your phone. PDFs and exports are rendered entirely on-device, and they carry no trackers, beacons, or web links.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no data sale. Not your financial data, not anyone's.
  • The only third party involved is Google Play — it delivers the app and processes the QuoteCentral Pro subscription. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

1. Who this policy is from

This policy describes how QuoteCentral ("QuoteCentral", "we", "the app") handles information. It applies to the QuoteCentral mobile app distributed on Google Play. It does not apply to websites, products, or services operated by other companies, even if QuoteCentral links to them or hands you to them (for example, when you share a PDF).

2. How QuoteCentral works

QuoteCentral is an offline-first quoting and invoicing app for one-person businesses. You keep a client list, quote jobs, turn accepted quotes into numbered invoices, record the payments that arrive, and hand your accountant an export. Everything the app does happens locally on your device:

  • Your records are stored in QuoteCentral's private, sandboxed database on your device.
  • PDF documents — quotes, invoices, credit notes, the accountant bundle — are rendered by an on-device printing engine. No web service sees them, and the documents contain no tracking pixels, beacons, or web links.
  • Search, numbering, tax calculations, reports, and exports are all computed on your device.
  • QuoteCentral operates with no developer-run servers, no user accounts, and no sign-in. There is no remote service for your data to reach, and no way for us to receive it. The app works in airplane mode, and in airplane mode nothing leaves the phone at all.

3. Information stored on your device

The following information exists only on your device, under your control. We do not have access to it, and deleting the app deletes it:

  • Your business profile: business name, contact details, tax registration words you enter, and the logo image you pick for your letterhead;
  • Your client list: names, contact details, and any addresses or notes you enter;
  • Quotes and quote lines, invoices and invoice lines, including tax setup per document;
  • Payments you record (cash, cheque, transfer), partial payments and deposits, client credit, and credit notes;
  • Document numbering sequences and the local audit trail that keeps them gap-free — including voided invoices, which are kept as watermarked records so every number has a document behind it;
  • Recurring invoice profiles, if you create any;
  • Reminder schedules you enable, and app settings such as region, currency, and theme;
  • Local quota counters (how many quotes, invoices, or exports you have used this month on the free tier);
  • Your QuoteCentral Pro entitlement, stored on-device so the app can unlock Pro features.

4. Information we do not collect

QuoteCentral does not collect, and the developer never receives:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account or registration details — there are no accounts;
  • Your clients' details, your quotes and invoices, or the payments you record;
  • Location, contacts, microphone audio, camera images, or browsing history;
  • Advertising or analytics identifiers, device fingerprints, or crash data sent to us;
  • Payment card numbers or bank details. The app records payments that you have collected — it never processes a card, touches a bank account, or takes a cut.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking code. No ads, no analytics on your financial data, nothing sold or shared — these are standing commitments of the product.

5. Permissions the app requests

QuoteCentral requests only the permissions it needs, and each is used only for the action you trigger:

PermissionWhy
Photos / media (read) To pick your business logo from your photos so it can appear on your letterhead. Only the image you select through the system picker is read; it is copied into the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.
Files / documents (read) To pick a backup file you previously exported, when you choose to restore. Only the file you select through the system picker is read.
Notifications If you turn on due-date reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device and delivered locally (clamped to waking hours). QuoteCentral sends no push notifications, because there is no server to send them.

That is the complete list. No camera, no location, no contacts, no microphone.

6. Purchases and payments

QuoteCentral Pro is sold through Google Play as an annual subscription. When you purchase:

  • Your payment is processed by Google Play's billing service under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your card number or payment details.
  • The app receives a purchase entitlement from Google Play so it can unlock Pro features on your device, and stores that entitlement on your device.
  • If you request a refund or have a billing problem, that is handled through Google Play. If you contact us about an order, Google's receipt is what you would show us — we cannot look your purchase up ourselves.

7. How your information is shared

We do not share your information with third parties for any purpose. There is no data broker, advertising network, analytics provider, or data sale anywhere in QuoteCentral. The only ways information ever moves off your device are ones you initiate:

  • Exports and sharing you choose. When you share or print a PDF, export a CSV, or build the accountant bundle, the app hands the file to your device's share sheet or print service, and it goes wherever you send it.
  • Backups you create. A backup is a file created on your device and handed to you. Where it is kept and who gets a copy is entirely up to you.
  • Email you choose to send. Support links hand a pre-drafted email to your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Google Play billing (described in section 6) — the one third-party service the app integrates with, used solely to process the Pro subscription.
  • Your device's backup, if you turned it on. If your device's built-in backup is enabled, your app data may be included in the backup kept in your Google account. That backup is controlled by your Google settings and covered by Google's privacy policy, not by us — and we never receive a copy.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained only for as long as the app is installed, and you are in control throughout:

  • You can delete clients and records inside the app at any time. Issued invoices you void are kept as watermarked records rather than erased, so your numbering stays gap-free and every number has a document behind it.
  • The Data & privacy screen shows live counts of everything stored and offers delete all local data, double-confirmed, which removes every record, setting, and entitlement from the database.
  • You can export everything first — CSV, PDFs, or the accountant bundle — and export is never locked away from you. On the free tier, core exports stay free; a lapsed or cancelled Pro subscription never locks your existing data. The app degrades to the free tier, and the limits apply only to new activity.
  • Uninstalling QuoteCentral removes the app and all of its data from your device.
  • Because we never receive your data, there is nothing held on our side to delete — deletion on your device is complete.

9. Security

Your records live in the app's sandboxed storage, which the operating system restricts to QuoteCentral. Because your data is never transmitted to or stored on any server we run, there is no server breach that could expose it. As with any data on a device, you should use your device's own security features (a screen lock; modern devices encrypt by default) to protect it. One honesty note: a backup file you export is an ordinary document containing your business records — the app does not add its own encryption to it, so treat a backup the way you would treat a paper ledger, and keep it somewhere safe. No security measure is absolute, and data on a physical device can be lost with the device — the export feature exists so you can keep your own backup.

10. Children

QuoteCentral is a general-audience business tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the app transmits nothing to anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — children included. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, contact us using the details below.

11. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, and whatever law applies to you (for example GDPR or CCPA rights of access, portability, correction, and deletion), in QuoteCentral you hold all of your data directly:

  • Access and portability: export your records (CSV, PDF, backup file, or the accountant bundle) from the app at any time.
  • Correction: edit any client, quote, invoice, or payment in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: delete records individually, use delete-all on the Data & privacy screen, or uninstall the app to delete everything.

If you have a request that the app cannot satisfy on its own, contact us and we will help — though in nearly every case the answer will be "that control is in the app itself," because there is nothing on our side.

12. International data handling

QuoteCentral can be used anywhere in the world, and its privacy design is location-independent: your business data never leaves your device, so no international transfers of your data by us take place. The only cross-border data movement associated with the app is the ordinary delivery of the app itself and its updates, and subscription billing, through Google Play's global infrastructure — none of which involves your business records.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling ever changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play. The standing commitments in this policy — no ads, no analytics on your financial data, nothing sold or shared, and export that is never locked away from you — are not negotiable and will not be walked back by a revision.

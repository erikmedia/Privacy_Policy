Slipbook Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026 · Applies to the Slipbook app on Google Play (package ca.slipbook.app) and the Slipbook app on the Apple App Store.

The short version

  • Slipbook has no servers and no accounts. Your receipts and everything derived from them are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • We do not collect your data. Nothing about you, your receipts, or your device is transmitted to us, because there is no us to transmit it to — the developer never receives a copy of anything you store in Slipbook.
  • Receipt scanning happens on your device. Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on-device. Receipt photos never leave your phone.
  • We do not show ads, run analytics, track you, or sell data. Not yours, not anyone's.
  • The only third party involved is Google Play, which processes purchases of Slipbook Pro. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

1. Who this policy is from

This policy describes how Slipbook ("Slipbook", "we", "the app") handles information. It applies to the Slipbook mobile app distributed on Google Play under the package name ca.slipbook.app and on the Apple App Store. It does not apply to websites, products, or services operated by other companies, even if Slipbook links to them.

2. How Slipbook works

Slipbook is an offline-first receipt tracker. You photograph receipts, import receipt files, or enter transactions manually, and the app organizes them into a searchable record book. Everything the app does happens locally on your device:

  • Your records are stored in Slipbook's private, sandboxed storage on your device.
  • Text recognition of receipt photos (OCR) is performed by an on-device text recognition engine bundled inside the app. The image is processed on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Search, budgets, splits, reports, and exports are all computed on your device.
  • Slipbook operates with no developer-run servers, no user accounts, and no sign-in. There is no remote service for your data to reach, and no way for us to receive it.

3. Information stored on your device

The following information exists only on your device, under your control. We do not have access to it, and deleting the app deletes it:

  • Receipt photos and thumbnails you capture or import;
  • Text extracted from those images by on-device OCR, and the fields you review or correct from it (merchant, amount, date and time, totals, tax, and tip);
  • Details you type in manually, including merchant names, amounts, and dates;
  • The last four digits of a payment card, only where a receipt shows them and you keep that record. Full card numbers are never asked for and never stored;
  • Splits and claimed amounts you record against a receipt, and your budgets and reports;
  • Files you import (for example, a PDF invoice), kept so you can view the original;
  • App settings and preferences, such as theme and your reminder schedule;
  • Local quota counters (for example, how many scans or imports you have used this month);
  • The date of your Slipbook Pro purchase, if you buy one, used to show your founding-supporter badge locally.

4. Information we do not collect

Slipbook does not collect, and the developer never receives:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account or registration details — there are no accounts;
  • Your receipts, receipt images, or their contents;
  • Precise or approximate location, contacts, microphone audio, or browsing history;
  • Advertising or analytics identifiers, device fingerprints, or crash data sent to us;
  • Payment card numbers or billing details (see section 6).

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking code. No ads, no analytics on financial content, nothing sold or shared — these are standing commitments of the product.

5. Permissions the app requests

Slipbook requests only the permissions it needs, and each is used only for the action you trigger:

PermissionWhy
Camera To photograph receipts when you choose to scan one. The photo is saved into your Slipbook record book on this device; it is never uploaded.
Photos / files (read) To import a receipt image or document you pick (for example, from your downloads or photo library). Only files you select through the system picker are read.
Biometrics / device lock If you turn on the app lock, Slipbook asks your device to verify your fingerprint, face, or screen lock before opening. Your biometric data is handled entirely by your device's operating system; Slipbook never sees or stores it.
Notifications If you set a receipt reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device and delivered locally. Slipbook sends no push notifications, because there is no server to send them.

6. Purchases and payments

Slipbook Pro is sold through Google Play (and the App Store on iOS). When you purchase:

  • Your payment is processed by Google Play's billing service under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your card number or payment details.
  • The app receives a purchase entitlement from Google Play so it can unlock Pro features on your device.
  • The app stores the purchase date on your device to display your founding-supporter badge. This date stays on your device and is transmitted nowhere.
  • If you request a refund or have a billing problem, that is handled through Google Play. If you contact us about an order, Google's receipt is what you would show us — we cannot look your purchase up ourselves.

7. How your information is shared

We do not share your information with third parties for any purpose. There is no data broker, advertising network, analytics provider, or data sale anywhere in Slipbook. The only ways information ever moves off your device are ones you initiate:

  • Google Play billing (described in section 6) — the one third-party service the app integrates with, used solely to process purchases.
  • Export and sharing you choose. When you export a CSV or share a receipt, the app hands the file to Android's share sheet or print service and it goes wherever you send it.
  • Email you choose to send. Support links hand a pre-drafted email to your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Your device's backup, if you turned it on. If Android's built-in backup is enabled for your device, your app data may be included in the device backup kept in your Google account. That backup is controlled by your Google settings and covered by Google's privacy policy, not by us — and we never receive a copy.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • You can delete any receipt or record in the app at any time; it is removed from your device's storage.
  • You can export everything first (CSV plus receipt images) — export remains available to you, and is never locked away from you, whatever happens to a subscription.
  • Uninstalling Slipbook removes the app and all of its data from your device.
  • A lapsed or cancelled Slipbook Pro subscription never locks your data. The app degrades to the free tier; your existing receipts, images, search, and exports stay available, and the limits apply only to new activity.
  • Because we never receive your data, there is nothing held on our side to delete — deletion on your device is complete.

9. Security

Your records live in the app's sandboxed storage, which Android restricts to Slipbook. Slipbook offers an optional app lock using your device's fingerprint, face, or screen lock. Because your data is never transmitted to or stored on any server we run, there is no server breach that could expose it. As with any data on a device, you should use your device's own security features (a screen lock, device encryption, which modern Android enables by default) to protect it. No security measure is absolute, and data stored on a physical device can be lost if the device is lost — the export feature exists so you can keep your own backup.

10. Children

Slipbook is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the app transmits nothing to anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — children included. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, contact us using the details below.

11. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, and whatever law applies to you (for example GDPR or CCPA rights of access, portability, correction, and deletion), in Slipbook you hold all of your data directly:

  • Access and portability: export your records (CSV and receipt images) from the app at any time.
  • Correction: edit any record in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: delete records individually, or uninstall the app to delete everything.

If you have a request that the app cannot satisfy on its own, contact us and we will help — though in nearly every case the answer will be "that control is in the app itself," because there is nothing on our side.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling ever changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play. The standing commitments in this policy — no ads, no analytics on financial content, nothing sold or shared, and no feature ever moved behind a new paywall in a way that takes away something already bought — are not negotiable and will not be walked back by a revision.

# Privacy Policy for Pocket Barn

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Pocket Barn** ("the game", "the app") handles information. The short version: **Pocket Barn does not collect, share or sell your personal information.**

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the game:

- does **not** ask you to create an account or sign in;
- does **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera;
- does **not** contain advertising, analytics or tracking software;
- does **not** request an internet connection or any other special permission on your device, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue playing, the game saves the following **only on your own device**:

- your farm and game progress (for example your land, crops, money, points, orders, machines and the current day and season);
- your settings (for example the graphics quality you chose and your opening-animation choice).

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is stored in the app's private storage, so it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data in your device settings.

## 3. Information collected by Google

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect certain information when you download and use apps, such as device and crash data for users who have chosen to share it with Google (for example through Android vitals). This is done by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us. You can read it at <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and manage these choices in your Google account and device settings.

## 4. Sharing of information

We have no personal information about you, so we do not share, sell, rent or trade any. The game does not include third-party software that collects information.

## 5. Children's privacy

Pocket Barn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to delete or to request deletion of.

## 6. Security

Your game progress is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Because no data is sent anywhere, there is no server-side information to be exposed.

## 7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal information about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete or export. You can remove all data the game has stored by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your device settings. If you have any questions or requests about privacy, contact us at the address above.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if optional rewarded ads, analytics or online features are added in the future), we will update this policy **before** that change is released, update the "Effective date" above, and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent. The current version is always available at the address where you found this policy.
